1.1 Chessboard ko jaano

Squares, files, ranks, center

Yaad rakho

-   Board 8×8 = 64 squares. Light on right: neeche right corner (h1) hamesha
    light.
-   Files = khadi lines a-h, ranks = aadi lines 1-8. Square ka naam = file +
    rank (e4).
-   Center = d4 e4 d5 e5. Center ke pieces zyada squares control karte hain.
-   White hamesha pehli chaal chalta hai.

Board: 64 squares

Chessboard mein 8×8 = 64 squares hote hain: 32 light, 32 dark.

Golden rule: board aise rakho ki right side ke neeche wala corner light square
ho. ("Light on right")

White ke pieces neeche (rank 1-2) aur Black ke upar (rank 7-8) se shuru hote
hain. White hamesha pehli chaal chalta hai.

1.2 Pieces kaise chalte hain

King, queen, rook, bishop, knight, pawn

Yaad rakho

-   Rook seedha (5), bishop tirchha (3), queen dono (9), knight "L" shape (3),
    king 1 square, pawn aage 1 (pehli baar 2).
-   Sirf knight pieces ke upar se kood sakta hai.
-   Pawn seedha chalta hai par tirchha capture karta hai, aur kabhi peeche
    nahi jaata.
-   King attack wale square par kabhi nahi ja sakta.
-   Center mein pieces sabse taakatwar: knight center mein 8 squares, corner
    mein sirf 2.

1.3 Special moves

Castling, en passant, promotion

Yaad rakho

-   Castling: king 2 square rook ki taraf, rook doosri taraf. O-O kingside,
    O-O-O queenside.
-   Castling tab nahi: king/rook pehle chal chuke, beech mein piece, king
    check mein, ya king attacked square se guzre.
-   En passant: dushman pawn 2 square chal kar bagal mein aaye to turant agli
    chaal mein capture.
-   Promotion: aakhri rank par pawn queen/rook/bishop/knight banta hai. Kabhi
    knight (underpromotion) best hota hai.

Castling: king ki suraksha

Castling mein ek hi chaal mein king 2 square rook ki taraf jaata hai, aur rook
king ke doosri taraf aa jaata hai.

-   Kingside: O-O (king g1, rook f1)
-   Queenside: O-O-O (king c1, rook d1)

Shart: king aur rook pehle kabhi na chale hon, beech mein koi piece na ho,
king check mein na ho, aur king jis square se guzre ya pahunche woh attack
mein na ho.

1.4 Check, checkmate, stalemate

Game kaise jeette hain

Yaad rakho

-   Check se bachne ke 3 tareeke: king hatao, block karo, attacker ko capture
    karo.
-   Checkmate = check + bachne ka koi raasta nahi = jeet.
-   Stalemate = check nahi par koi legal chaal nahi = draw. Jeette waqt iska
    dhyan rakho!
-   Back-rank mate: king apne hi pawns ke peeche band.

Check

Jab king par attack ho to use check kehte hain. Check se turant bachna zaroori
hai. 3 tareeke:

-   King ko hatao safe square par
-   Beech mein piece rakho (block)
-   Attack karne wale piece ko capture karo

1.5 Draw ke rules

Kab game barabar khatam hota hai

Yaad rakho

-   Draw ke 5 tareeke: stalemate, 3-fold repetition, 50-move rule,
    insufficient material, agreement.
-   K+N vs K aur K+B vs K = draw (mate namumkin).
-   K+R vs K aur K+Q vs K = jeet.
-   Haarti position mein repetition / perpetual check se draw bacha sakte ho.

Draw ke 5 tareeke

-   Stalemate: chaal hai, par koi legal move nahi aur check bhi nahi.
-   3-fold repetition: same position 3 baar aaye (same player ki chaal ke
    saath).
-   50-move rule: 50 chaalein (dono taraf) bina pawn move ya capture ke.
-   Insufficient material: mate karna namumkin. Jaise King vs King,
    King+Bishop vs King, King+Knight vs King.
-   Agreement: dono players draw maan lein.

1.7 Notation: moves likhna aur padhna

Chess ki bhasha

Yaad rakho

-   Chaal = piece letter + square: Nf3, Bc4. Pawn ka letter nahi: e4.
-   x capture, + check, # mate, O-O / O-O-O castling, e8=Q promotion.
-   ! achhi, !! shandaar, ? galti, ?? blunder, !? interesting, ?! doubtful.
-   Fool's mate seekh: opening mein f aur g pawns faltu mat chalao.

Algebraic notation

Har chaal = piece ka letter + square.

-   K = King, Q = Queen, R = Rook, B = Bishop, N = Knight
-   Pawn ka koi letter nahi: e4 matlab pawn e4 par gaya
-   x = capture: Nxe5 (knight ne e5 par capture kiya), exd5 (e-pawn ne d5 par)
-   + = check, # = checkmate
-   O-O = kingside castle, O-O-O = queenside
-   e8=Q = promotion

Annotation: ! achhi chaal, !! shandaar, ? galti, ?? blunder, !? interesting,
?! doubtful.

Box method

Yaad rakho

-   Queen ko dushman king se knight ki doori par rakho: box chhota karte jao.

2.4 Hanging pieces aur safety

Attackers vs defenders

Yaad rakho

-   Beginners zyada games pieces free mein dene se haarte hain.
-   Har chaal se pehle: koi piece bina protection? Opponent ki pichli chaal ne
    kya attack kiya?
-   Attackers > defenders = piece khatre mein.
-   Opponent ka hanging piece dikhe to turant saza do.

Blunder se bacho

Beginners zyada games pieces free mein dene se haarte hain, galat plan se
nahi.

Har chaal se pehle:

-   Kya meri chaal ke baad koi piece bina protection ke reh jaayega?
-   Opponent ki pichli chaal ne kya attack kiya?
-   Kisi square par attackers gino aur defenders gino. Attackers zyada = piece
    khatre mein.

3.2 Pin

Piece hil nahi sakta

Yaad rakho

-   Pin: aage wala piece hil nahi sakta kyunki peeche zyada keemti piece hai.

3.6 Mate patterns (zaroor yaad karo)

Back rank, smothered, Anastasia, Arabian, Boden

Yaad rakho

-   Strong players mate pattern pehchante hain, har baar calculate nahi karte.
-   Back-rank, smothered, Anastasia, Arabian, Boden: shakal yaad karo.
-   Legal's mate: pinned knight se queen sacrifice, teen minor pieces se mate.
-   Greek gift Bxh7+: tab jab f6 par black knight na ho aur Ng5 safe ho.
-   Philidor's legacy: Qg8+!! Rxg8 Nf7# smothered mate.

Patterns = shortcuts

Strong players har mate ko calculate nahi karte, woh pattern pehchante hain.
Yeh patterns yaad karo:

-   Back-rank mate: king apne pawns ke peeche band.
-   Smothered mate: king apne hi pieces se ghira, knight mate deta hai.
-   Anastasia mate: knight + rook, king kinare par.
-   Arabian mate: rook + knight corner mein.
-   Boden mate: do bishops criss-cross.
-   Legal mate: opening trap, queen sacrifice.
-   Greek gift: Bxh7+ sacrifice castled king par.

3.7 Aur tactics: zwischenzug, x-ray, interference

Advanced motifs

Yaad rakho

-   Zwischenzug: recapture se pehle ek zyada forcing chaal.
-   X-ray: piece dushman piece ke aar-paar square control karta hai.
-   Interference: do dushman pieces ke beech apna piece rakh kar connection
    kaato.
-   Clearance, desperado, perpetual check: ye bhi yaad rakho.

Advanced motifs

-   Zwischenzug (in-between move): expected recapture ki jagah pehle ek zyada
    forcing chaal (check/threat) khelo.
-   X-ray: piece dushman piece ke aar-paar ek square ko control karta hai.
-   Interference: do dushman pieces ke beech mein apna piece rakh kar unka
    connection kaato.
-   Clearance: apne hi piece ko hata kar line/square khaali karo.
-   Desperado: jo piece waise bhi jaane wala hai, use jitna ho sake mehenga
    becho.
-   Perpetual check: haarti position mein lagaataar check de kar draw.

5.1 King aur pawn: opposition

5.3 Rook endgames: Lucena aur Philidor

Sabse common endgame

Yaad rakho

-   Aadhe endgames rook endgames hote hain.
-   Lucena (jeet): rook 4th rank par la kar bridge banao.
-   Philidor (draw): rook 6th rank par; pawn aage aaye to rook peeche jaa kar
    checks.
-   Tarrasch rule: rook passed pawn ke peeche. Active rook > passive rook.

Rook endgames sabse zyada aate hain

Lagbhag aadhe endgames mein rooks hote hain. 2 positions zaroor yaad karo:

-   Lucena (jeet): pawn 7th rank par, king uske aage. Technique: "bridge
    banao" rook se.
-   Philidor (draw): defending side ka rook 3rd rank (apni taraf se 6th) par,
    jab tak pawn aage na aaye. Pawn aaya to rook peeche jaa kar check dete
    raho.

Aur rule: rook passed pawn ke peeche rakho (Tarrasch rule), aage nahi. Active
rook passive rook se behtar.

5.4 Minor pieces aur theory

Good/bad bishop, wrong corner, kaun jeetta hai

Yaad rakho

-   K+Q vs K, K+R vs K jeet. K+B vs K, K+N vs K, K+N+N vs K draw.

-   Open board, dono kinaron par pawns = bishop behtar. Band position =

-   Opposite color bishops: aksar draw.

Kaunse endgames jeet, kaunse draw

-   K+Q vs K, K+R vs K: jeet
-   K+B+N vs K: jeet par mushkil (corner us color ka jo bishop control karta
    hai)
-   K+N+N vs K: draw (force nahi kar sakte)
-   K+B vs K, K+N vs K: draw
-   K+Q vs K+R: aksar jeet (technique lambi)
-   K+R vs K+B, K+R vs K+N: aksar draw
-   K+R+P vs K+R: depends: Lucena jeet, Philidor draw
-   Opposite color bishops + pawns: aksar draw chahe ek-do pawn zyada ho

6.1 Pawn structure

Isolated, doubled, backward, passed, chains

Yaad rakho

-   Pawn moves permanent hain: structure decide karta hai pieces kahan achhe.
-   IQP: kamzor pawn, par active pieces. IQP wala attack kare, doosra trade
    kare.
-   Backward pawn ke aage ka square ek hole banta hai.
-   Pawn chain: base par attack karo (Nimzowitsch).

Pawns = position ki haddi

"Pawns are the soul of chess" (Philidor). Pawns peeche nahi jaate, isliye har
pawn move permanent hai. Pawn structure decide karta hai pieces kahan achhe
honge.

Isolated pawn (IQP)

d4 pawn ke bagal (c, e files) mein koi apna pawn nahi: isolated.

-   Kamzori: pawn se protect nahi hota, aur uske aage d5 square blockade ke
    liye perfect (Black knight d5 par).
-   Taakat: active pieces, d5 break aur kingside attack.

6.4 Plan kaise banayein

Imbalances aur attack

Yaad rakho

-   Plan imbalances se banta hai: material, minor pieces, pawn structure,
    space, files, king safety.
-   Jahan strong ho wahan khelo.
-   Opposite side castling = pawn storm race; same side castling mein
    center/queenside khelo.
-   Minority attack (b4-b5): Carlsbad structure mein c6 par kamzor pawn
    banana.

Imbalances dekho

Plan banane ke liye farq (imbalances) dekho:

-   Material: kaun aage?
-   Minor pieces: bishop vs knight, bishop pair?
-   Pawn structure: kamzor pawns, passed pawns, majorities
-   Space: kiske paas zyada jagah?
-   Open files / weak squares
-   Development aur king safety

Phir: jahan tum strong ho wahan khelo. Space zyada hai to us taraf attack
karo. Kamzori hai to use theek karo ya trades se bacho.

6.5 Prophylaxis aur restriction

Opponent ka plan pehle roko

Yaad rakho

-   Har chaal se pehle: "Opponent kya chahta hai?" Use pehle roko.
-   Misaal: h3 (...Bg4 roko), Kh1 (checks se pehle hi bacho).
-   Restriction: dushman pieces ke achhe squares cheen lo.

Prophylaxis

Prophylaxis (Nimzowitsch, Karpov, Petrosian): apni chaal se pehle poocho

-   h3 khelna taaki ...Bg4 pin na ho
-   Rook ko us file par rakhna jo khulne wali hai
-   King ko pehle hi safe square (Kh1) par le jaana taaki baad mein checks na
    aayein

Restriction: dushman pieces ke achhe squares cheen lo. Jo piece hil nahi
sakta, woh piece nahi.

7.1 Deep calculation

Grandmaster ki tarah sochna

Yaad rakho

-   Candidate moves ka tree banao, har branch ek baar.
-   Kotov rule: line baar-baar mat dekho, ek baar gehra dekho aur faisla karo.
-   Forcing line ke beech quiet moves mat bhoolo.
-   Aakhri check: opponent ka sabse forcing jawab kya hai?

Calculation ke pro tips

-   Tree banao: candidate moves → har ek ke 1-2 best jawab → aage. Har branch
    ek baar, saaf.
-   Kotov rule: ek line ko baar-baar mat dekho. Ek baar gehra dekho, faisla
    karo.
-   Comparison: aksar perfect calculation ki zaroorat nahi, bas kaunsi line
    behtar hai.
-   Quiet moves ko mat bhoolo: forcing line ke beech ek shaant chaal bhi hoti
    hai.
-   Blunder check (aakhri 10 second): "Opponent ka sabse forcing jawab kya
    hai?"
-   Intuition + calculation: pehle feel, phir calculation se check.

Training: roz 15-30 min tough puzzles bina board chhuye solve karo, poori line
dimaag mein.

7.2 Time management

Clock bhi opponent hai

Yaad rakho

-   Classical 60+ min, rapid 10-60, blitz ≤10, bullet <3. +2 = har chaal par 2
    second.
-   Opening jaldi, time critical positions mein lagao.
-   Ek chaal par total time ka 10% se zyada kam hi.
-   Time trouble mein simple, safe chaalein.

Clock ka khel

Time controls (FIDE):

-   Classical: har player 60+ minute (jaise 90 min + 30 sec/move)
-   Rapid: 10 se 60 minute
-   Blitz: 10 minute ya kam (jaise 3+2, 5+0)
-   Bullet: 3 minute se kam (online)

Increment (+2): har chaal ke baad 2 second judte hain.

Tips:
- Opening jaldi (jo aata hai), sabse zyada time critical positions mein (jab
plan badle, ya tactics ho).
- Ek chaal par total time ka 10% se zyada kam hi lagao.
- Time trouble se bacho: simple, safe chaalein.
- Opponent ke time mein bhi socho (general plans).

7.3 Tournament rules aur ratings

OTB khelne ke liye zaroori

Yaad rakho

-   Touch-move: chhua piece chalana padega. Theek karna ho to "adjust" /
    "J'adoube".
-   Clock usi haath se dabao jisse chaal chali. Phone bajna = aksar haar.
-   Draw offer apni chaal ke baad, clock se pehle.
-   Titles: CM 2200, FM 2300, IM 2400 + norms, GM 2500 + 3 norms.

Over-the-board rules

-   Touch-move: jo piece chhua, wahi chalana padega. Apna piece theek karna ho
    to pehle "adjust" (ya "J'adoube") bolo.
-   Piece chhod diya = chaal poori.
-   Clock usi haath se dabao jisse chaal chali.
-   Illegal move: rapid/blitz mein opponent ko extra time; doosri illegal move
    par haar (FIDE rules ke hisaab se).
-   Classical mein notation likhna zaroori (scoresheet).
-   Phone pass mein ho aur baj jaaye = aksar haar.
-   Draw offer apni chaal chalne ke baad, clock dabane se pehle.

Rating: Elo system. Jeet se rating badhti hai, haar se ghatti. Stronger player
ko harane par zyada points.

FIDE titles: CM (2200), FM (2300), IM (2400 + norms), GM (2500 + 3 GM norms).
Women: WCM, WFM, WIM, WGM.

7.4 Psychology aur practical play

Dimaag ko control karo

Yaad rakho

-   Haar ke baad gussa (tilt) = break lo.
-   Stronger opponent se mat daro, normal achhi chaalein.
-   Jeeti position mein relax mat ho; haarti position mein resist karo.
-   Neend, paani, fitness aur warm-up routine.

Mental game

-   Tilt: ek haar ke baad gusse mein games mat khelo. Break lo.
-   Stronger opponent: darr ke draw mat khelo, normal achhi chaalein. Rating
    board par nahi khelti.
-   Jeeti position: relax mat ho. "The hardest game to win is a won game."
    Safe moves, counterplay rokna.
-   Haarti position: resist karo! Complications, traps, time pressure:
    comeback hote hain.
-   Draw offers: position aur tournament situation dekh kar.
-   Fitness, neend, paani: lambe games mein bahut fark padta hai.
-   Routine: game se pehle halka warm-up (puzzles), phone door.

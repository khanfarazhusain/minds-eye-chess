Level 1

Shuruaat: board aur pieces

Rules, pieces ki chaal, special moves, check-mate, notation

Yahan se chess ki neev banegi. Har cheez board par khud kar ke seekhoge. Jaldi mat karo: yeh basics pakke hue to aage sab aasaan lagega.

1.1

Chessboard ko jaano

Squares, files, ranks, center

Yaad rakho
  • Board 8×8 = 64 squares. Light on right: neeche right corner (h1) hamesha light.
  • Files = khadi lines a-h, ranks = aadi lines 1-8. Square ka naam = file + rank (e4).
  • Center = d4 e4 d5 e5. Center ke pieces zyada squares control karte hain.
  • White hamesha pehli chaal chalta hai.

Board: 64 squares

Chessboard mein 8×8 = 64 squares hote hain: 32 light, 32 dark.

Golden rule: board aise rakho ki right side ke neeche wala corner light square ho. ("Light on right")

White ke pieces neeche (rank 1-2) aur Black ke upar (rank 7-8) se shuru hote hain. White hamesha pehli chaal chalta hai.

Files aur ranks

Khadi lines (upar-neeche) ko files kehte hain: a se h tak.

Aadi lines (left-right) ko ranks kehte hain: 1 se 8 tak.

Har square ka naam = file + rank. Jaise peeli line e-file hai, aur us par ek square hai e4.

Center sabse important

Beech ke 4 squares d4 e4 d5 e5 ko center kehte hain.

Center mein baitha piece zyada squares control karta hai. Isliye opening mein sab center ke liye ladte hain.

Diagonal = tirchhi line, jaise a1 se h8.

Sawal Kaunsa square light hai?

Jawab

✓ h1

"Light on right": White ki taraf right corner h1 light hota hai. a1 dark hai.

Sawal e4 mein e kya batata hai?

Jawab

✓ File (khadi line)

Pehle letter = file (a-h), phir number = rank (1-8).

1.2

Pieces kaise chalte hain

King, queen, rook, bishop, knight, pawn

Yaad rakho
  • Rook seedha (5), bishop tirchha (3), queen dono (9), knight "L" shape (3), king 1 square, pawn aage 1 (pehli baar 2).
  • Sirf knight pieces ke upar se kood sakta hai.
  • Pawn seedha chalta hai par tirchha capture karta hai, aur kabhi peeche nahi jaata.
  • King attack wale square par kabhi nahi ja sakta.
  • Center mein pieces sabse taakatwar: knight center mein 8 squares, corner mein sirf 2.

Rook ♜

Rook seedha chalta hai: upar, neeche, left, right. Jitne marzi squares, jab tak koi raste mein na aaye.

Value: 5 points.

Board par dekho Rook d4 kahan-kahan ja sakta hai?

Rook d7 ke knight ko capture kar sakta hai, par uske aage nahi ja sakta. Capture karne ke liye dushman ke square par jaate hain.

Bishop ♝

Bishop sirf diagonal (tirchha) chalta hai. Light square wala bishop hamesha light squares par hi rahega.

Value: 3 points.

Board par dekho Bishop c4

Apna pawn b3 raasta rok raha hai. f7 ka black pawn capture ho sakta hai.

Queen ♛

Queen = rook + bishop. Seedha bhi, tirchha bhi. Sabse taakatwar piece.

Value: 9 points. Isko faltu mein mat khona!

Board par dekho Queen d4

Center mein queen 27 squares tak control karti hai!

Knight ♞

Knight "L" shape mein chalta hai: 2 square ek taraf, phir 1 square side mein.

Knight hi akela piece hai jo doosre pieces ke upar se kood sakta hai. Har chaal mein woh light se dark (ya dark se light) square par jaata hai.

Value: 3 points.

Board par dekho Knight d4

Center mein knight 8 squares, corner mein sirf 2. "Knight on the rim is dim": kinare par knight kamzor hota hai.

Board par dekho Corner wala knight a1: kitne squares?

Sirf 2! Isliye knights ko center ki taraf lao.

Pawn ♟

Pawn sirf aage chalta hai, ek square. Pehli chaal mein 2 square bhi ja sakta hai.

Par capture tirchha karta hai (ek square aage diagonal). Seedha aage koi ho to pawn ruk jaata hai.

Pawn kabhi peeche nahi jaata. Value: 1 point.

Board par dekho Pawn e2 kahan ja sakta hai?

e3, e4 (pehli chaal) aur d3 par knight ko capture.

King ♚

King har taraf sirf 1 square chalta hai.

King ko kabhi aise square par nahi le ja sakte jahan dushman attack kar raha ho. Yahan black rook poori 5th rank control kar raha hai.

King ki value infinite hai: king gaya to game gaya.

Board par dekho King e4 kahan ja sakta hai?

d5, e5, f5 par rook ka attack hai, isliye king wahan nahi ja sakta.

Sawal Kaunsa piece doosre pieces ke upar se kood sakta hai?

Jawab

✓ Knight

Sirf knight jump karta hai.

Sawal Pawn capture kaise karta hai?

Jawab

✓ Tirchha aage (diagonal)

Pawn seedha chalta hai par tirchha capture karta hai.

1.3

Special moves

Castling, en passant, promotion

Yaad rakho
  • Castling: king 2 square rook ki taraf, rook doosri taraf. O-O kingside, O-O-O queenside.
  • Castling tab nahi: king/rook pehle chal chuke, beech mein piece, king check mein, ya king attacked square se guzre.
  • En passant: dushman pawn 2 square chal kar bagal mein aaye to turant agli chaal mein capture.
  • Promotion: aakhri rank par pawn queen/rook/bishop/knight banta hai. Kabhi knight (underpromotion) best hota hai.

Castling: king ki suraksha

Castling mein ek hi chaal mein king 2 square rook ki taraf jaata hai, aur rook king ke doosri taraf aa jaata hai.

  • Kingside: O-O (king g1, rook f1)
  • Queenside: O-O-O (king c1, rook d1)

Shart: king aur rook pehle kabhi na chale hon, beech mein koi piece na ho, king check mein na ho, aur king jis square se guzre ya pahunche woh attack mein na ho.

Abhyaas

Kingside castle karo (O-O). King ko g1 par tap karo.

Jawab dekho

1. O-O

King ab safe corner mein hai aur rook center ki taraf aa gaya.

Sawal Kya White yahan kingside castle kar sakta hai?

Jawab

✓ Nahi: f1 attack mein hai

King f1 se guzrega, aur c4 ka bishop f1 ko attack kar raha hai. Isliye castling allowed nahi.

En passant

Agar dushman ka pawn 2 square chal kar tumhare pawn ke bilkul bagal mein aa jaaye, to tum use aise capture kar sakte ho jaise woh 1 hi square chala ho.

Ise en passant kehte hain. Yeh sirf turant agli chaal mein hi ho sakta hai.

Yahan Black ne abhi d7-d5 khela.

Abhyaas

En passant karo: e5 pawn se d6 par capture.

Jawab dekho

Pawn d6 par gaya aur d5 ka black pawn board se hat gaya.

Promotion

Pawn jab aakhri rank (White ke liye 8th) par pahunchta hai, to woh queen, rook, bishop ya knight ban jaata hai. Zyadatar queen banate hain.

Isliye endgame mein pawns bahut keemti ho jaate hain.

Abhyaas

Pawn ko queen banao.

Jawab dekho

1. e8=Q

Ye bhi sahi: e8=R

Ab White ke paas queen hai, aur yeh jeetne wali position hai.

Abhyaas

Kabhi queen nahi, knight banana better hota hai! Sabse achhi chaal dhundo.

Jawab dekho

e8=N+ check deta hai aur c7 ki queen par bhi attack karta hai (fork). King hatega, phir knight queen le lega. Ise underpromotion kehte hain.

1.4

Check, checkmate, stalemate

Game kaise jeette hain

Yaad rakho
  • Check se bachne ke 3 tareeke: king hatao, block karo, attacker ko capture karo.
  • Checkmate = check + bachne ka koi raasta nahi = jeet.
  • Stalemate = check nahi par koi legal chaal nahi = draw. Jeette waqt iska dhyan rakho!
  • Back-rank mate: king apne hi pawns ke peeche band.

Check

Jab king par attack ho to use check kehte hain. Check se turant bachna zaroori hai. 3 tareeke:

  • King ko hatao safe square par
  • Beech mein piece rakho (block)
  • Attack karne wale piece ko capture karo

Checkmate = jeet

Agar king check mein hai aur bachne ka koi tareeka nahi, to yeh checkmate hai. Game khatam, jisne mate diya woh jeeta.

Yahan Black king ke apne pawns hi uska raasta rok rahe hain. Ise back-rank mate kehte hain.

Abhyaas Mate in 1

Mate in 1: ek chaal mein checkmate karo!

Jawab dekho

1. Ra8#

Ra8#: back-rank mate. King ke paas koi square nahi.

Abhyaas Mate in 1

Mate in 1: queen aur king ki team-work.

Jawab dekho

1. Qb8#

King escape squares band karta hai, queen 8th rank par check deti hai: Qb8#.

Stalemate = draw!

Agar jiski chaal hai uska king check mein nahi, par usse koi bhi legal chaal nahi hai, to yeh stalemate hai. Stalemate = draw.

Yahan Black ki chaal hai: king h8 hilega to attack mein aayega, aur koi aur piece nahi. Jeeta hua game draw ho gaya! Jeetne wale ko is se bachna hai.

Sawal Black ki chaal hai. Yeh kya hai?

Jawab

✓ Stalemate

Black king check mein nahi hai par uski har chaal attack mein jaati hai. Koi aur piece nahi, to stalemate (draw).

Sawal Check se bachne ka kaunsa tareeka galat hai?

Jawab

✓ Apni koi bhi doosri chaal chalna

Check mein ho to sirf woh chaal chal sakte ho jo check hataye.

1.5

Draw ke rules

Kab game barabar khatam hota hai

Yaad rakho
  • Draw ke 5 tareeke: stalemate, 3-fold repetition, 50-move rule, insufficient material, agreement.
  • K+N vs K aur K+B vs K = draw (mate namumkin).
  • K+R vs K aur K+Q vs K = jeet.
  • Haarti position mein repetition / perpetual check se draw bacha sakte ho.

Draw ke 5 tareeke

  • Stalemate: chaal hai, par koi legal move nahi aur check bhi nahi.
  • 3-fold repetition: same position 3 baar aaye (same player ki chaal ke saath).
  • 50-move rule: 50 chaalein (dono taraf) bina pawn move ya capture ke.
  • Insufficient material: mate karna namumkin. Jaise King vs King, King+Bishop vs King, King+Knight vs King.
  • Agreement: dono players draw maan lein.

Sawal King + ek Knight vs akela King: kya hoga?

Jawab

✓ Draw: mate ke liye material kam hai

Akele knight ya bishop se mate nahi diya ja sakta.

Sawal King + Rook vs akela King?

Jawab

✓ Jeet: rook se mate diya ja sakta hai

Rook (ya queen) ke saath mate force kar sakte hain. Yeh technique Level 2 mein seekhoge.

Sawal Dono players ek hi chaal baar-baar repeat karte hain aur position 3 baar same hoti hai. Kya hoga?

Jawab

✓ Draw (3-fold repetition)

3 baar same position = draw claim kar sakte hain. Isko "perpetual check" mein bhi dekhoge.

Kab lena hai, kab nahi

Yaad rakho
  • Pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, king anmol.
  • Trade se pehle points jodo: 3 dekar 5 mila = exchange jeeta.
  • Har chaal mein dekho: koi piece hanging (bina protection) to nahi?
  • Capture se pehle attackers aur defenders gino.

Point system

  • Pawn = 1
  • Knight = 3
  • Bishop = 3 (thoda zyada jab dono bishops ho)
  • Rook = 5
  • Queen = 9
  • King = anmol

1.7

Notation: moves likhna aur padhna

Chess ki bhasha

Yaad rakho
  • Chaal = piece letter + square: Nf3, Bc4. Pawn ka letter nahi: e4.
  • x capture, + check, # mate, O-O / O-O-O castling, e8=Q promotion.
  • ! achhi, !! shandaar, ? galti, ?? blunder, !? interesting, ?! doubtful.
  • Fool's mate seekh: opening mein f aur g pawns faltu mat chalao.

Algebraic notation

Har chaal = piece ka letter + square.

  • K = King, Q = Queen, R = Rook, B = Bishop, N = Knight
  • Pawn ka koi letter nahi: e4 matlab pawn e4 par gaya
  • x = capture: Nxe5 (knight ne e5 par capture kiya), exd5 (e-pawn ne d5 par)
  • + = check, # = checkmate
  • O-O = kingside castle, O-O-O = queenside
  • e8=Q = promotion

Annotation: ! achhi chaal, !! shandaar, ? galti, ?? blunder, !? interesting, ?! doubtful.

Sawal Bxf7+ ka matlab?

Jawab

✓ Bishop ne f7 par capture kiya aur check diya

B = bishop, x = capture, + = check.

Sawal O-O-O kya hai?

Jawab

✓ Queenside castle

Teen O = lamba castle (queen ki taraf).

Sawal exd5 mein kaunsa piece chala?

Jawab

✓ e-file ka pawn

Pawn capture mein uski file likhte hain: e-pawn ne d5 par capture kiya.

2.1

Queen se mate (K+Q vs K)

Box method

Yaad rakho
  • Queen ko dushman king se knight ki doori par rakho: box chhota karte jao.
  • King edge par aa jaaye to apna king paas lao.
  • Mate queen + king ki team se hota hai.
  • Har chaal ke baad check karo: Black ke paas chaal bachi hai? Warna stalemate.

Box method

Queen + King se mate dene ke steps:

  • Queen ko knight ki doori par rakho dushman king se. Isse king ek "box" mein band hota hai.
  • Box ko chhota karte jao, jab tak king kinare (edge) par na aa jaaye.
  • Ab apne king ko paas lao.
  • Queen se mate do, king ki help se.

Khatra: stalemate! Har chaal ke baad dekho ki Black ke paas koi chaal bachi hai.

Final picture

Aisi position mein mate hota hai: queen king ke saamne, aur apna king queen ko protect kar raha hai. Ya queen poori edge rank par check de jabki king saamne khada ho.

Khud khelo Checkmate karo

Ab tum karo: Black king ko 20 chaal ke andar mate do.

Queen ko king ke paas mat le jao jahan woh protect na ho!

2.2

Rook se mate (K+R vs K)

Opposition aur rook ki deewar

Yaad rakho
  • Rook ek deewar banata hai: king ko ek taraf band karo.
  • Apne king ko saamne lao (beech mein ek square) = opposition.
  • Kings aamne-saamne hon tab rook se check: king peeche jaata hai.
  • Rook ko dushman king se door rakho taaki woh capture na ho.

Rook ki deewar

Rook ek deewar banata hai jo king ko ek taraf band rakhti hai.

  • Rook se king ko ek rank/file mein band karo.
  • Apne king ko dushman king ke saamne lao (beech mein ek square) = opposition.
  • Jab kings aamne-saamne hon, rook se check do: king peeche jaayega.
  • Aise edge tak dhakelo, phir edge par check = mate.

Rook ko dushman king se door rakho taaki woh use pakad na le.

2.3

Do rooks ki seedhi (ladder mate)

Sabse aasaan mate

Yaad rakho
  • Do rooks seedhi ki tarah: ek rank band, doosra agli rank par check.

Ladder mate

Do rooks seedhi ki tarah chalte hain: ek rook ek rank band karta hai, doosra agli rank par check deta hai. Phir pehla aage badhta hai.

King ki zaroorat nahi! Bas dhyan rahe ki king rooks ke paas aa kar unhe capture na kar le: tab rook ko door (doosre kinare) le jao.

2.4

Hanging pieces aur safety

Attackers vs defenders

Yaad rakho
  • Beginners zyada games pieces free mein dene se haarte hain.
  • Har chaal se pehle: koi piece bina protection? Opponent ki pichli chaal ne kya attack kiya?
  • Attackers > defenders = piece khatre mein.
  • Opponent ka hanging piece dikhe to turant saza do.

Blunder se bacho

Beginners zyada games pieces free mein dene se haarte hain, galat plan se nahi.

Har chaal se pehle:

  • Kya meri chaal ke baad koi piece bina protection ke reh jaayega?
  • Opponent ki pichli chaal ne kya attack kiya?
  • Kisi square par attackers gino aur defenders gino. Attackers zyada = piece khatre mein.

Sawal Black ka knight c6 attack mein hai. Kitne White pieces use attack kar rahe hain aur kitne Black defend?

Jawab

✓ 1 attacker, 1 defender

Bb5 attack karta hai, b7 pawn defend karta hai. Barabar: abhi khatra nahi. Lekin bishop ne knight ko pin kiya hai (Level 3).

Abhyaas

Tum Black ho. White ne Nxe5 khel kar pawn liya, par knight ab hanging hai. Saza do!

Jawab dekho

1… Nxe5

Nxe5: knight (3) ke badle sirf pawn (1) mila White ko. Attackers aur defenders ginna kabhi mat bhoolo.

Abhyaas

White ki chaal. Black ne bishop g4 par rakha, par woh protected nahi hai.

Jawab dekho

1. hxg4

hxg4: pawn (1) ne bishop (3) le liya. Black ne h3 pawn ka attack nahi dekha.

2.5

Opening ke 10 principles

Pehli 10-12 chaalein kaise khelein

Yaad rakho
  • Center lo (e4/d4), knights aur bishops develop karo, jaldi castle.
  • Ek piece baar-baar mat chalao, queen jaldi mat nikaalo.
  • Rooks connect karke open files par lao.
  • f7/f2 sabse kamzor square: Scholar's mate se bacho (...g6 ya ...Qe7).

Opening principles

  • Center lo: e4, d4 (ya e5, d5) pawns se.
  • Pieces develop karo: knights aur bishops ko pehle bahar lao. Knights pehle, bishops baad mein.
  • Jaldi castle karo (pehli 10 chaal mein).
  • Ek piece ko baar-baar mat chalao bina wajah.
  • Queen ko jaldi mat nikaalo: chhote pieces use bhaga dete hain aur tum time khote ho.

3.7

Aur tactics: zwischenzug, x-ray, interference

Advanced motifs

Yaad rakho
  • Zwischenzug: recapture se pehle ek zyada forcing chaal.
  • X-ray: piece dushman piece ke aar-paar square control karta hai.

Advanced motifs

  • Zwischenzug (in-between move): expected recapture ki jagah pehle ek zyada forcing chaal (check/threat) khelo.

  • Perpetual check: haarti position mein lagaataar check de kar draw.

6.1

Yaad rakho

Pawns = position ki haddi

"Pawns are the soul of chess" (Philidor). Pawns peeche nahi jaate, isliye har pawn move permanent hai. Pawn structure decide karta hai pieces kahan achhe honge.

Isolated pawn (IQP)

d4 pawn ke bagal (c, e files) mein koi apna pawn nahi: isolated.

  • Kamzori: pawn se protect nahi hota, aur uske aage d5 square blockade ke liye perfect (Black knight d5 par).
  • Taakat: active pieces, d5 break aur kingside attack.

IQP wala attack kare, doosra pieces trade karke endgame mein jaaye.

Doubled aur backward pawns

Doubled pawns (ek file par do): aksar kamzor, par open file deta hai.

Black ...c5 se d4 (base) par vaar karta hai. White kingside par (f4-f5) attack karta hai, kyunki chain ka sira us taraf ishaara karta hai.

  • Touch-move: jo piece chhua, wahi chalana padega. Apna piece theek karna ho to pehle "adjust" (ya "J'adoube") bolo.
  • Piece chhod diya = chaal poori.
  • Clock usi haath se dabao jisse chaal chali.
  • Illegal move: rapid/blitz mein opponent ko extra time; doosri illegal move par haar (FIDE rules ke hisaab se).
  • Classical mein notation likhna zaroori (scoresheet).
  • Phone pass mein ho aur baj jaaye = aksar haar.
  • Draw offer apni chaal chalne ke baad, clock dabane se pehle.

Rating: Elo system. Jeet se rating badhti hai, haar se ghatti. Stronger player ko harane par zyada points.

FIDE titles: CM (2200), FM (2300), IM (2400 + norms), GM (2500 + 3 GM norms). Women: WCM, WFM, WIM, WGM.

Engine ka tareeka (har chaal par)

  1. Khatra: opponent agli chaal mein kya karna chahta hai?
  2. Latakte mohre: kaunsa mohra bina bachaav ke hai, mera ya uska?
  3. Checks, captures, threats: saari forcing chaalein dhoondo.
  4. Candidates: checklist A-G se 2-3 chaalein chuno (forcing, khatre se bachao, mohra sudhaaro, pawn break, castling, open file, center). Har ek ka opponent ka sabse achha jawab socho.
  5. Final: jo chaal har jawab ke baad bhi achhi rahe, wahi khelo.
